Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our auto transport services

How much does car shipping cost?

Pricing depends on distance, route, vehicle size, and shipping type. We offer competitive rates with zero hidden charges.

Is my vehicle insured during transport?

Absolutely. Every shipment includes full insurance coverage for your peace of mind.

Can I put personal items in my car?

A small amount (under 100 lbs) is typically allowed, but personal items are not insured — we recommend keeping valuables separate.

Do I need to pay upfront?

No. You only pay once your carrier has been assigned and confirmed.

How long does delivery take?

Transit times vary by route — short-distance moves take 1–3 days, while cross-country shipments may take 5–9 days.

Will my vehicle be tracked?

Yes. Our support team provides updates throughout the entire transport process.
